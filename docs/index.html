Hands-free Android control

Let your phone
read your hands.

Use the front camera to recognize waves and pinches, then perform swipes, clicks, and long presses through Android accessibility. Control your phone without touching it.

隔空操作，
不被屏幕限制。

本地运行的手势识别工具，专为需要免触操作、辅助操作或更自由手机控制的人设计。

↔

左右挥手

右挥、左挥可分别绑定上滑、下滑、点击或长按。

⌁

捏合手势

拇指与食指捏合释放，触发可配置的手机操作。

◌

后台识别

前台服务接管摄像头，退到后台后仍可继续识别。

◒

性能模式

自动、省电、标准、高响应，温度过高时自动降档。

⌖

坐标可调

滑动、点击、长按位置支持百分比或像素单位。

◎

完全本地

不联网、不上传数据，摄像头画面不保存、不录制。

快速开始

  1. 安装 APK 并授予相机权限。
  2. 在系统“无障碍”设置中开启隔空助手。
  3. 打开应用，将手对准前置摄像头。
  4. 右挥默认上滑，左挥默认下滑，可在设置中修改。

Hands-free control,
without limits.

A local gesture recognition tool for touch-free interaction, accessibility, and more natural control of your Android phone.

↔

Left & right waves

Bind right and left waves to swipe up, swipe down, click, or long press.

⌁

Pinch gesture

Pinch and release with your thumb and index finger to trigger an action.

◌

Background recognition

A foreground service keeps camera recognition running in the background.

◒

Performance modes

Auto, battery saving, standard, and high response modes with thermal fallback.

⌖

Adjustable coordinates

Configure swipe, click, and long-press positions in percentages or pixels.

◎

Local by design

No network, no uploads, and no saved or recorded camera frames.

Quick start

  1. Install the APK and grant camera permission.
  2. Enable Air Assistant in system Accessibility settings.
  3. Open the app and face the front camera with your hand.
  4. Right wave swipes up and left wave swipes down by default; both are configurable.
